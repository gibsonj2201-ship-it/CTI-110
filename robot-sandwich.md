 # My PB&J My Way

 ## Asumptions
- robot will put bread together without ingredients in the middle
- robot will leave bread on the counter
## ingredients
 - 2 slices whole wheat bread
 - 2 teaspoon peanut butter
 - 2 teaspoon grape jelly
 
 ## things you will need  
 - spoon

 ## Instructions
 - pick up the 2 slices of bread
 - put the 2 slices of bread on the counter 
 - seperate the 2 slices of bread 
 - grab ther jar of peanut butter 
 - put the jar on the counter 
 - open the jar 
 - grab the spoon  
 - scoop out the exact amount of peanut butter listed
 - spead on one slice of bread 
 - grab jar of jelly 
 - place jar on the counter 
 - using the same spoon scoop out exact amount of jelly listed 
 - spread jelly on other slice of bread 
 - put spoon in sink 
 - pick up both slices of bread 
 - face sides of bread with spead on them towards each other 
 - now put them together 
 - put sandwich back on the counter 

## done when
- sandwich is on the counter
